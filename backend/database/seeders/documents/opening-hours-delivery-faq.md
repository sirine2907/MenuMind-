# Opening Hours & Delivery FAQ

## Where are you located?

Dar El Menu is at 12 Rue Habib Bourguiba, La Marsa, Tunis, five minutes on foot from La Marsa Plage TGM station. Free parking is available on Avenue Taieb Mhiri after 19:00.

## What are your opening hours?

Monday to Thursday we serve lunch from 12:00 to 15:00 and dinner from 19:00 to 23:00. Friday to Sunday we are open all day, from 12:00 to 23:30. The kitchen takes its last order 30 minutes before closing.

During Ramadan we open at sunset for ftour (iftar) and stay open until 01:00 for shour. Ramadan hours are announced on our social media a week in advance.

We are closed on Eid al-Fitr and on the first day of Eid al-Adha.

## Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver to La Marsa, Sidi Bou Said, Carthage, Gammarth and Les Berges du Lac. Delivery runs during opening hours, until 22:30 on weekdays and 23:00 on weekends.

## How much does delivery cost?

Delivery costs 3 TND per order and is free for orders of 60 TND or more. The minimum order for delivery is 20 TND. Prices on the menu are in Tunisian dinars (TND) and include VAT.

## How long does delivery take?

Most deliveries arrive within 35 to 50 minutes. On Friday and Saturday evenings it can take up to 60 minutes. You can follow your order status (pending, confirmed, being prepared, ready, delivered) in the Orders page or by asking the MenuMind assistant.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, bank cards (Visa, Mastercard and local CIB cards) and D17 mobile payment. Delivery riders carry a card terminal. We do not accept cheques.

## Can I cancel or change my order?

You can cancel an order free of charge while it is still pending. Once the kitchen has confirmed it, please call us on +216 71 740 123 and we will do our best to help.

## Do you take reservations?

Yes, for groups of up to 12 people, by phone or WhatsApp on +216 71 740 123. For larger groups and private events (weddings, engagements, corporate dinners) please contact us at least one week ahead.

## Do you have a children's menu?

We serve half portions of the couscous, the Djerbian rice and the Margherita pizza at 60% of the regular price for children under 10.
