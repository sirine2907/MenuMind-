# Allergen & Dietary Policy

Dar El Menu cooks everything to order in a single kitchen. We take allergies seriously and we would rather lose a sale than make a guest ill. This policy explains how we handle the 12 allergens we track and what we can and cannot guarantee.

## Allergens we track

Every dish on our menu is labelled with the allergens it contains: gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, dairy, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame and molluscs. The labels are kept up to date by our head chef whenever a recipe changes, and the same data powers our online menu and the MenuMind assistant.

## Peanuts and tree nuts

Peanuts are used in only two recipes: the Couscous Mesfouf (sweet couscous topped with roasted peanuts) and the Mhalbia pudding. Both are prepared on a dedicated dessert station, away from the hot line. Tree nuts (almonds, pistachios, pine nuts, walnuts) appear in the baklawa, the mint tea with pine nuts and the Mediterranean cheese salad.

We never cook with peanut oil. All frying, including brik and fricassé, is done in sunflower oil.

Even so, because nuts are handled in the same kitchen, we cannot guarantee that any dish is 100% free of nut traces. Guests with a severe (anaphylactic) peanut or nut allergy should speak to the manager on duty before ordering.

## Gluten

Our couscous, breads, brik pastry (malsouka), pizzas and most desserts contain wheat. Grilled fish, the Slata Mechouia, Ommek Houria, Kamounia and the Djerbian rice are naturally made without gluten, but they are cooked near flour, so they are suitable for gluten avoidance and not certified for coeliac disease.

## Cross-contamination

We use colour-coded boards and separate utensils for seafood and for allergen-free orders. The fryer is shared between brik (egg, fish) and fricassé (gluten, egg, fish). When you declare an allergy we flag the ticket and the dish is plated by the chef, not by the line.

## Vegetarian and vegan dishes

Dishes marked vegetarian contain no meat, poultry or fish, but may contain eggs, dairy or honey. Dishes marked vegan contain no animal products at all. Our vegetable couscous is cooked in a vegetable broth, never in a meat stock.

## How to tell us about an allergy

Mention it when you order, at the table, on the phone or in the order notes online. The MenuMind assistant will always check allergen information against our live menu before recommending a dish, and it will remind you to confirm with staff for severe allergies.
